# Contributing

## Build

How to build is the comment at the top of the justfile, and Build from
source in README.md. `just` lists every recipe.

`just test` runs the tests under ASan.

## Commits

A subject is `package: imperative explainer`, as the git log shows.
One is `journal: add leita journal on, off, status and run`.

Do not add a `Co-authored-by` trailer. Do not attribute a commit to an
agent.

## Pull requests

Every pull request needs the Contributor Licence Agreement in CLA.md
signed before merge. You sign with your GitHub account by commenting on
the pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

CLA.md section 11 accepts that comment as your signature. It covers each
contribution you submit while the agreement is in force. You may stop it
covering future contributions by written notice to Librium. Licences
already granted stay in force.

For a substantial contribution, Librium may ask you to sign by hand or
with a qualified electronic signature. You may choose to sign that way.
